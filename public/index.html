How it holds

Three things, in order

  1. First

    Everyone agrees on the time

    Each browser measures its own offset from the room's clock, keeping the fastest reading of the last eight rather than the average.

  2. Then

    Nobody starts until everyone can

    Press play and the room waits for each person to buffer, then starts everyone on the same future timestamp. Anyone still loading gets pulled in after.

  3. Throughout

    Corrections you can't hear

    Falling behind speeds your playback up by a few percent instead of jumping the video. No stutter, no jolt, and nothing audible.

Bring content you have the right to watch — your own files, your own media server, YouTube, or the public domain. Rooms are private and unlisted, and codes are the only way in.
