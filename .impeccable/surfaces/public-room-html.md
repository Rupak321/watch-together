## Scope

The room (`public/room.html`, `public/room.css`, `public/room.js`), its name screen, the offline page and the install sheet. Visitor mode: Operate. The home page is out of scope and keeps its current look.

## Audience and job

Friends on phones, long-distance couples on laptops or a TV browser, families on mixed devices — equally. The job: see who is here and talking, put one film in front of everyone, start it together, and keep talking without losing the picture.

## Constraints

- One screen on every device and orientation: the page never scrolls, nothing overflows or clips; long lists scroll inside their own region.
- Every existing feature keeps working; the scripts' element ids and classes survive.
- Too-dark, cluttered, dull and plain were the reported faults of the old room.

## Memorable moment

Whoever is talking lends their colour to the live ring around the picture.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The room is a group chat wrapped around one shared picture. It refuses the streaming app's black sidebar and the page that scrolls to reach chat.

OWN-WORLD: Night-ink ground with paper-white type and white pill actions. Every person owns one colour, carried through their face ring, name, bubbles and reactions. State colours are law: green in sync or ready, amber catching up or buffering, red behind, lost or muted. Rounded bubbles, avatar rings, one set of drawn line icons, the system sans.

STORY: People see who is here, pick a film, press play together, and keep the thread going beside the picture.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: slim top bar; the picture at full 16:9 on the left with faces and a floating control bar; a thread column on the right with Chat, People, Watch and Sound tabs and the composer pinned. Phone upright: picture on top, a now-playing strip, tabs, the thread filling the rest. Sideways: the picture fills; the thread slides in as a drawer. Before a film, the stage shows the room code at monumental scale and one next step. Nothing scrolls.

FORM: The Group Thread, candidate 4 of 7, seed key 5b64c4d6. Raised by terminal wayfinding (monumental code, one next step), arcade cabinet (state colour law), alphabet storm (one reaction moment at a time), tensegrity (four named sync states) and racing livery (the speaker's colour on the live ring). Signature interaction: the live ring. Motion: 160–220 ms state changes only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
